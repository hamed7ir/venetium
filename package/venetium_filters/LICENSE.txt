Venetium's built-in ad and tracker blocker list (venetium_filters\filters.dat)

filters.dat is built from these two filter lists, unmodified, by Chromium's ruleset_converter
(components/subresource_filter/tools), which keeps their network-request rules:

  EasyList     https://easylist.to/easylist/easylist.txt
  EasyPrivacy  https://easylist.to/easylist/easyprivacy.txt

The list versions are in version.txt next to this file.

To report a site the lists break, or an ad they miss, use https://easylist.to/ (forums and GitHub
issue tracker). In Venetium, "Always allow ads on this site" in the blocked-ads bubble, or the
site's "Ads" setting, turns the blocker off for that site; "Any site you visit can show any ad" in
chrome://settings/content/ads, or --disable-features=SubresourceFilter, turns it off everywhere.
